# Using DoubleDragon

## Record a spin

Click a number on the board, or type a number between 0 and 36 in the entry field and choose **Record** (or press Enter). The selected cell increments its count and the spin is inserted at the left of the history strip.

## Correct an entry

Choose **Undo** to remove the most recent spin. This refreshes board counts, the recent-history strip, and group statistics.

## Group cards

The top card row is **1st Dozen**, **2nd Dozen**, and **3rd Dozen**. The second row is **Column 1**, **Column 2**, and **Column 3**.

Every card reports:

- **hits**: recorded spins belonging to that group.
- **absent**: consecutive spins since that group last appeared.

## Settings

- **Global Trigger**: the shared absence threshold for all six groups. Once a group has been absent for at least this many spins, its Start betting button becomes available.
- **Stop Loss**: the last Fibonacci step allowed for an active group. Its menu labels both the step and its stake, such as `Step 5 - 5U`.

`U` means the configured unit size. The displayed Fibonacci values are units, not currency.
